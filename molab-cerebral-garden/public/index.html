How this garden works

Gestures

👍 Thumbs up — bloom outward
👎 Thumbs down — wilt inward
☝️ Point up — spring! flowers rise up all over the screen
✌️ Victory — the whole garden blooms fully and straightens into order
✋ Open palm — full-screen bloom, your lines rearrange into smiley faces
✊ Closed fist — your lines vanish, the flowers wither
🤟 I-love-you — everything goes beautifully messy; lines dance like jazz
These map to MediaPipe's 7 built-in hand gestures — there's no literal ":)" or "✕" hand-shape detector, so the closest matches are used.
